# Home Movie Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026.

Home Movie is provided by the developer of the [home-movie project](https://github.com/falconRUBIO/home-movie). This policy covers the Home Movie iPhone app.

## Recordings and projects

Video, audio, thumbnails, imported looks, editing settings, and project information are stored on your device. Home Movie has no account system, advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics service. It does not send your recordings or project information to a developer server.

## Permissions

Camera access lets you record video. Microphone access records sound with your scenes. Photos permission is add-only: it saves copies you request or original-scene copies if you enable that option. The app does not request permission to read your Photos library. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a bounded local support log and receives performance and crash reports from Apple's MetricKit framework. Reports can contain technical device information, operation events, and stack traces. The app does not automatically transmit these reports to the developer. Share Diagnostics opens the system share interface so you can choose whether and where to send them. Apple's own diagnostic collection and services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## Sharing, Photos, Files, and backups

When you export or share, you choose the destination. That destination handles the copy under its own policies. Project backups contain original recordings and project metadata, including recoverable deleted items. iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, and device backups depend on your Apple settings; the app does not verify completed cloud synchronization.

## Retention and deletion

Projects remain locally until you delete them. Recently Deleted retains footage until you choose Delete Permanently. Local diagnostic records can be cleared in Diagnostics. Temporary exports and thumbnails may be cleared by the app. Copies already saved to Photos, Files, backups, or other destinations remain separate and must be deleted there. Deleting the app can remove its local projects; keep external backups of footage you want to retain.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the email contact on our [support page](https://home-movie-support.falcon-craft-4475.chatgpt.site/). If you email support, the information you choose to send and your email address are used to respond to your request. Email is handled by email service providers. Please avoid sending sensitive recordings or diagnostics unless needed; you can request deletion of support correspondence by email. This document will be updated when the app's privacy practices change.

## Support website

This website provides support and privacy information without requiring an account. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider processes requests needed to deliver the pages. Contact links open your email app; this website does not submit a support form or automatically send diagnostics.
